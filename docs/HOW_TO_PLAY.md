# How to play Sol 72

Keep four astronauts alive for 72 hours (Cadet) or 96 hours (Commander) on Mars or the Moon.

**Pick your Sun** on the menu: today's real flares from NASA, a famous storm (2017, 2024, 2003), the 2018 Mars dust storm, or random events.

**The trade-off:** power, oxygen, food, shielding and morale compete. Sheltering from radiation protects the crew but costs morale and stops the rover; the generator costs fuel; shielding costs power. No choice is free.

**Controls:** WASD or arrows to walk, tap the ground to move, drag to look, tap objects or press **E** near them. Keys **1 to 9** run the nine actions (1 rover, 2 clean panels, 3 repair, 4 grow food, 5 ration, 6 generator, 7 shelter, 8 shielding, 9 eco life support). Space pauses, M mutes.

**Need help?** The light-bulb tip at the top says what needs attention right now.

**Flares:** a flare is spotted first and its dangerous particles arrive about 4 hours later. In Commander some flares erupt on the far side of the Sun and come with no warning at all.

**Stars:** survive, reach the research goal (drive the rover in daylight), and finish with a well-run base.

**What kids learn:** radiation dose and why shielding matters, why Mars crews cannot ask Earth for help in real time, and that power is life.
