Mars Outpost

NASA Space Apps Challenge

Keep the outpost alive.

Mars gives you dust, radiation and cold nights. You give it air, power and food. Every fix costs something somewhere else.

How to play
  1. Each day and each night you get a few action points. Spend them to repair, upgrade, research or allocate power.
  2. Then end the turn. Mars responds with real-data weather: dust storms cut solar power, radiation wears the shield, equipment breaks.
  3. You cannot fix everything. Watch the outlook, choose what matters most, and keep air, power, food and shield above the mission goals until the relief ship arrives.
